Tempo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July 2026 · Last updated: 3 August 2026

Tempo is a personal screen-time tracker. It automatically measures how long you spend in the apps and on the websites you use, and shows those statistics back to you. This policy explains exactly what Tempo records, where it is stored, and your rights over it.

Who is responsible for your data (the "controller"):
Aleksandar Drejer Petersen, Denmark.
Contact: aleksandardrejer@gmail.com

1. What Tempo is

Tempo automatically measures how long you spend in the apps and on the websites you use, and syncs those statistics to your own password-protected Tempo account so you can see them across your devices. Your account and data are hosted on our behalf by Supabase, in the EU (Ireland) (see §5). A copy is also kept on your own device so the app works offline.

2. What Tempo records

For each app and website you use, Tempo records:

  • The app's program name (e.g. chrome.exe, code.exe).
  • The website's domain-based name (e.g. YouTube, Onshape) — derived from the site's domain, not the full web address.
  • Time counts per day, per device: how many seconds each app/site was Open, Focused, and Active (in use). "Active" requires keyboard/mouse input within the last 3 minutes, or audio / a call in progress.
  • Your device name (your computer's host name), to separate stats per device.

We also process:

  • Your email address (to create and secure your account).
  • Payment details — handled entirely by our payment processor Stripe; we never see or store your card number. We store only a Stripe customer reference and your paid-through date.
  • Limited beta product milestones, such as whether signup was completed, the app was opened, onboarding was completed, the browser extension connected, or a PDF was exported. These events can include the operating system and app version, but never app names, website names, window titles, URLs, time totals, or work contents. We use them only to understand where setup fails and whether the beta is useful.

3. What Tempo does NOT collect

Tempo is deliberately coarse. It does not record, store, or transmit:

  • Keystrokes, mouse movement, or clipboard contents;
  • Screenshots or screen contents;
  • The full URLs of pages you visit, or page content;
  • The titles of your windows or browser tabs — these are read momentarily on your device only, to identify the site and detect audio, and are then discarded; they are never saved or uploaded;
  • The contents of your files, messages, or emails.

We never sell your data, never share it for advertising, and never build cross-user profiles.

4. The browser extension

The optional Tempo browser extension exists only to tell Tempo which website is in the foreground, so time in your browser is attributed to the right site instead of just "the browser". It sends the active tab's domain, title, and an audio-playing flag only to Tempo running on your own computer (127.0.0.1, a local address). It has no external network access and cannot send anything to us or anyone else. As described in §2, Tempo stores only the domain-based site name from this — not the title or URL.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure: Tempo's use of information received from browser APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Browser-tab information is used only to provide the extension's clearly described website time-tracking feature. It is not used for advertising, credit decisions, or profiling; is not sold; is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the Tempo service described in this policy; and is not read by humans except when required for security, legal compliance, or support requested by the user. The extension does not transmit browser-tab information to Tempo's cloud service. The desktop app converts the local report into the domain-based time totals described in §2 before any sync.

5. Where your data is stored

  • Your account and synced statistics are stored with Supabase, in the EU (Ireland) region, on our behalf.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe.
  • Account emails (sign-in, password reset) are delivered via Google (Gmail SMTP).

These providers are our data processors; they may only process your data to provide these services to us, and may not use it for their own purposes.

6. Why we process it, and the legal basis (GDPR)

We are based in the EU and process your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • To provide the product you asked for — tracking and showing your stats, and syncing them to your account — on the basis of performance of our contract with you.
  • To secure your account and process payment, on the basis of contract and our legitimate interest in a working, fraud-resistant service.
  • To understand whether the limited beta setup succeeds and improve it, on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and improving Tempo, using only the product milestones described in §2.

7. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you close your account (or ask us to delete it), your synced statistics and profile are deleted. While an account is inactive or expired we may still receive queued uploads from your devices, but you cannot read them until the account is active again; deletion removes them. You can clear the local copy on your own device at any time.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Because the app is built around showing you your own data, most of this is available directly:

  • Access/export: all your data is visible in the app, and you can export a PDF report at any time.
  • Deletion: contact us at aleksandardrejer@gmail.com to delete your account and all synced data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority (in Denmark, Datatilsynet).

9. Security

Account sessions are protected with tokens stored in your operating system's secure credential store; we never store your password in plain text (Supabase stores only a salted hash). Cloud data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is restricted so that you can only read your own data.

10. Children

Tempo is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. International transfers

Your cloud data is stored in the EU (Ireland). Some processors (e.g. Stripe, Google) may process limited data outside the EU under appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify account holders by email.
